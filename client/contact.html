Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is AgriShare free to join for farmers?

Yes! Registering as a farmer and listing your tools or exploring resources is 100% free with zero registration fees or recurring charges.

2. How are payments handled for Nominal Rental?

To keep the platform simple, transparent, and accessible without expensive payment gateway deductions, nominal rental amounts are paid directly peer-to-peer (via UPI or cash) between farmers upon machine inspection and handover.

3. How does Barter Exchange work?

Farmers specify in their listing what they need in exchange (for example, lending a cultivator in exchange for 2 days of threshing labour or quality organic seeds). Both farmers confirm terms via the request workflow before exchange.

4. Can I switch the website language to Marathi?

Yes. Simply click the 🌐 मराठी toggle in the top navigation bar. All labels, buttons, categories, and dashboards will instantly translate into Marathi and remain saved during your session.

5. Can two farmers book the same tractor on overlapping dates?

No. The backend database automatically enforces date-conflict validation, rejecting overlapping approvals so farmers are never double-booked.
