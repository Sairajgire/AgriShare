FLEXIBLE PARTICIPATION

Three Flexible Sharing Models

Choose the model that best fits your farming schedule and requirements.

🎁

Free Sharing

Farmers lend tools, sprayers, and extra water pumps without any rental fee to support neighbours in need during critical crop windows.

🔄

Barter Exchange

Exchange farming equipment or services directly without money (e.g. use of a rotavator in exchange for organic seeds or threshing assistance).

🏷️

Nominal Rental

Rent high-value tractors and combine harvesters at heavily subsidized, affordable hourly or daily rates without middleman commission.
