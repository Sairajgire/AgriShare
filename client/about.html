MULTIDISCIPLINARY ENGINEERING MINOR PROJECT

About AgriShare

A web-based hyper-local platform designed to eliminate machinery shortages, reduce farmer debt, and foster community-driven agricultural resource exchange.

Our Mission & Problem Context

In contemporary Indian agriculture, small and marginal farmers comprise more than 85% of operational landholdings. Despite their vital role in food security, high capital costs make essential machinery like 45+ HP tractors, laser land levellers, combine harvesters, and boom sprayers economically inaccessible.

Simultaneously, medium and large farmers own modern machinery that sits idle in sheds for up to 250 days every year. AgriShare bridges this gap by creating an organized hyper-local sharing platform featuring Free Sharing, Barter Exchange, and Nominal Subsidized Rental.

Academic Focus: Developed for the subject MDM – Remote Sensing and GIS as a practical engineering minor project illustrating how spatial locality, community networks, and full-stack software architecture combine to address real-world rural challenges.
